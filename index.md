# Mohsen Danaie

:::{div}
:class: msc-tagline
Automated scanning electron nanobeam diffraction, and the statistical
characterisation of materials that it makes possible.
:::

## About

I am a Senior Electron Microscopy Scientist at
[ePSIC](https://www.diamond.ac.uk/Instruments/Imaging-and-Microscopy/ePSIC.html),
the electron Physical Science Imaging Centre at Diamond Light Source, the UK
national synchrotron. My work combines scanning electron nanobeam diffraction
with automated analysis workflows and script-controlled acquisition on the
microscope, applied to engineering alloys, energy materials, catalysts, and
beam-sensitive specimens. Earlier work measured hydrogen storage in magnesium
hydrides, the corrosion of magnesium alloys, and radiation damage in perovskites
and superconductors.
